MANDARIN PARENTING COMICS · SEASON 1

One real moment at home.Simple ideas you can try today.

Bilingual Chinese and English comics from ZHIYULE about the small challenges families meet when speaking Mandarin at home. Tap any page to read it full size. Season 1 has 9 issues, released one by one.

Issue 01

My child understands Mandarin, so why do they reply in English?

You speak Mandarin and your child switches straight to English? Don’t jump to conclusions yet.

What this issue covers

  • Understanding and speaking are different skills. Replying in English may come down to fluency, habit or confidence. One answer in English doesn’t mean your child doesn’t want to learn Mandarin.
  • Method 1: Turn Mandarin into a fun game. Start with the toys and games your child loves. Don’t push them to speak; make them want to.
  • Method 2: When your child speaks English, respond to the meaning first. Reply to what they want to say, then model the Mandarin sentence naturally. There’s no need to make them repeat it straight away.
  • Method 3: Follow their interest and keep the chat going. Add your own observations, imagination and experiences, so the conversation flows back and forth like passing a ball.
  • Create a Mandarin-friendly home. Use Mandarin at meals, brushing teeth and getting dressed, read picture books together, and make time to tell stories and act them out.

A child’s willingness to speak matters more than perfect sentences.

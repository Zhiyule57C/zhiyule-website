Privacy notice

How we handle the details you send through this site.

Operating company

Language and music preferences

Browser local storage remembers your language and music preferences; session storage keeps the playback position. Enquiry form entries are not stored.

What the enquiry form collects

When you send a trial enquiry we receive: the parent’s name, phone / WhatsApp number, child’s age, programme of interest, Mandarin experience, your question and the page you sent it from. Please do not include NRIC numbers, health details or other sensitive information.

Purpose

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a trial and follow up about our classes. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Storage and service providers

Enquiries are sent through Google (Apps Script and Google Sheets) and kept in ZHIYULE’s company account, and an email notification goes to hello@zhiyule.com.sg. Only staff handling enquiries can see them.

Copying and WhatsApp

The copy button writes your draft to the clipboard when clicked. Choosing to open WhatsApp passes the draft to WhatsApp through a link. Sending remains your choice.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to enrolment are deleted within 12 months of our last contact. Enrolled families’ records are kept for as long as needed to provide our classes.

Access, correction and withdrawal

To access, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, email hello@zhiyule.com.sg or WhatsApp +65 8071 9508. Photos and recordings are covered by a separate consent.

YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Reluctant to write, or unsure how to talk about a picture?

Begin with something the child wants to express. Making art gives them a story to tell; hands-on construction invites attention to structure and gives writing a meaningful context.

THE APPROACH AT THIS STAGE

Creative arts × character components & spatial construction

Creative expression extends picture-book themes, connecting character components and spatial relationships with fine-motor work. Children practise expressing their ideas in Mandarin and telling stories in a clear sequence.

SMALL SIGNS TO NOTICE

Small steps worth celebrating.

  • Explore character components and spatial structures
  • Practise fine-motor control and writing
  • Describe personal creations with richer ideas and narrative

These are learning goals to work towards. Every child develops at their own pace.

YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Anxious about separation, or not ready to respond in Mandarin?

We allow time to observe, approach and join in. Music and play bring familiarity; a glance, an imitation or a softly sung sound is worth noticing.

THE APPROACH AT THIS STAGE

Orff-inspired music & movement × multisensory exploration

Rhythm, songs, age-appropriate objects and textures connect Mandarin sounds with everyday words. PG begins with music and sensory experience rather than picture-book themes, character learning or writing.

SMALL SIGNS TO NOTICE

Small steps worth celebrating.

  • Join interactions around familiar Mandarin sounds
  • Respond through gestures, emerging words or songs
  • Build early vocabulary connected to everyday experience

These are learning goals to work towards. Every child develops at their own pace.

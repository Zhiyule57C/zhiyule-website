WHY ZHIYULE

Joyful Learning. Purposeful Teaching. Meaningful Progress.

We believe joyful learning and strong academic foundations belong together. At ZHIYULE, child-centred experiences are thoughtfully connected to clear Mandarin learning goals.

01

Joyful Beginnings, Strong Foundations

Music, stories, drama and interactive activities spark curiosity while progressively building vocabulary, character recognition, reading and writing skills.

02

Every Child Learns Differently

We recognise individual language backgrounds, learning rhythms and participation styles, providing age-appropriate guidance and support.

03

Five Stages of Progressive Learning

Our PG–K2 pathway develops listening, speaking, reading and writing skills, building a foundation for Primary One Mandarin.

04

Making Progress Visible

Classroom observations and periodic feedback help families understand their child’s learning journey.

05

Expertise Behind Every Lesson

Our curriculum is led by Qianhui, drawing on extensive experience in Singapore education and curriculum development, supported by evolving teacher training and quality standards.

THE GROWTH APPROACH

Every Joyful Experience Is Backed by Thoughtful Teaching.

At ZHIYULE, music, storytelling, drama and sensory activities are purposeful learning experiences that support language comprehension, expression and retention.

Our GROWTH teaching framework brings together meaningful participation, language development, positive interaction and progressive learning, creating classrooms that are both engaging and academically purposeful.

MEET QIANHUI

Built on Educational Experience. Designed for Every Child’s Growth.

ZHIYULE was founded by Qianhui, an educator with over 15 years of experience in Singapore education, including drama education, Mandarin curriculum development and teacher training.

She believes children should never have to choose between joyful learning and academic progress. Through thoughtful curriculum design and an understanding of child development, children can build both language competence and confidence.
